Frequently Asked Questions

What platforms does Hearted support?

Hearted currently supports saving content from X (Twitter), Pinterest, Instagram, and Midjourney. More platforms are coming soon.

How do I save content?

There are two ways to save: click the heart icon that appears on supported content, or use the keyboard shortcut ⌘ + ⇧ + H (Mac) to instantly save what you're viewing.

Where is my data stored?

All your saved content is stored locally on your device. Hearted does not upload your data to any cloud service. Your archive syncs across your Apple devices via iCloud if enabled.

Is Hearted free?

Yes, Hearted will be completely free to download and use. There are no in-app purchases or subscriptions planned.

When will Hearted be available?

Hearted is currently in development. Sign up on our homepage to be notified when it launches for iOS and macOS.

Does Hearted work on iPhone and Mac?

Yes! Hearted includes both iOS and macOS apps with Safari extensions. Your archive syncs across devices via iCloud.

How do I delete my archive?

You can delete individual items from within the app, or delete all data by removing the app from your device. To also remove iCloud data, delete the Hearted folder from iCloud Drive.
