Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2025

Your privacy matters. Hearted is designed with privacy as a core principle.

Data Collection

We don't collect your data. Hearted does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information to our servers. We have no servers. Your archive lives entirely on your device.

What Hearted Stores

When you save content with Hearted, the following is stored locally on your device:

  • The content you choose to save (images, text, links)
  • Metadata about saved items (source URL, save date)
  • Your app preferences and settings

This data never leaves your device unless you enable iCloud sync.

iCloud Sync

If you enable iCloud, your saved content syncs across your Apple devices using your personal iCloud account. This data is encrypted and handled according to Apple's privacy policies. We have no access to your iCloud data.

Safari Extension

The Hearted Safari extension only activates on supported platforms (X, Pinterest, Instagram, Midjourney). The extension:

  • Only reads content when you explicitly save it
  • Does not track your browsing activity
  • Does not inject scripts on unsupported websites
  • Does not communicate with any external servers

Analytics

Hearted does not include any analytics, tracking, or telemetry. We don't know how many users we have, how often you use the app, or what content you save.

Third Parties

Hearted does not share data with third parties because we don't have your data to share.

Data Deletion

To delete your data:

  • Individual items: Delete from within the app
  • All local data: Delete the app from your device
  • iCloud data: Delete the Hearted folder from iCloud Drive (Files app → iCloud Drive → Hearted)

Children's Privacy

Hearted does not knowingly collect information from children under 13. Since we don't collect any data, this concern is addressed by design.

Changes to This Policy

If we update this privacy policy, we'll post the new version here with an updated date. Since we don't collect contact information, we cannot notify you directly of changes.
